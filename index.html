Solo Defending

Alone at dawn. A legion by the end.

You are the Keeper, the last guardian of a small keep, with a lantern of teal fire and nobody else. Walk the land and build wherever the ground allows by day; fight beside your towers by night.

Every elite who falls to you leaves its essence behind. Bind it and it rises again on your side, with its own body and its own powers. Level after level, your defense becomes an army made of the enemies who came for you.
